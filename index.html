For CNC manufacturers

Turn suitable spare capacity into new work without publishing your direct contact details on a public directory.

Capability-first matching

Show machines, work envelope, materials, processes, tolerances, inspection and certifications. Buyers can evaluate capability without being handed your phone number or website.

Pay for successful work

The demo is designed around a simple “no job, no charge” principle. A production system can add a success fee or other commercial model later.

Controlled communication

Use CNC-HUB messaging. Contact details, URLs and obvious attempts to move the conversation off-platform can be detected and restricted.

Reputation that matters

Build a record of completed jobs, on-time delivery, customer ratings and verified certifications rather than relying only on a company description.

Trust without exposing the network.

CNC-HUB can be rich in technical information while remaining deliberately light on personally identifying and bypass-enabling information.

🔒

Anonymous early-stage profiles

Suppliers can appear as verified manufacturing partners with capability, region, rating and performance data rather than a public contact card.

🛡️

Document protection

Technical files can be watermarked and, in a production backend, scanned for names, addresses, email addresses and other identifying content before controlled release.

💬

Platform messaging

Keep the commercial conversation in one place and detect phone numbers, email addresses, URLs and common off-platform contact attempts.
